# Prompts — destroço da canoa (C1)

Anexe `canoa_f.png` (ou a canoa vazia) como referência, para a madeira sair igual. Salve como `destroco.png`.

## Bloco de estilo
```
Use the attached image ONLY as an art-style reference: same pixel density, same wood colors, 1-pixel dark outline, limited palette, top-down 3/4 view, light from the upper left. Pure white background, no ground shadow, no text, no numbers, no grid lines. Each item clearly separated by white space, all items at the same scale as the attached canoe.
```

## Prompt (1 folha, 1 linha de 6 itens)
```
Pixel art sprite sheet, 1 row of 6 items, made from the SAME wooden canoe of the attached image:
1 and 2: a single broken wooden plank floating flat on the water, about 34x9 pixels, jagged splintered ends, a short piece of rope tied around it, two frames of a gentle bob (item 2 tilted one pixel and with a slightly different water ripple line around it);
3: a few small broken canoe pieces floating (2 short splintered planks and a broken piece of the paddle), spread out, about 40x20 pixels;
4: the same broken pieces washed up on dry sand, lying flat, about 40x18 pixels;
5: the broken canoe hull split in two halves lying on the beach sand, wet dark wood, about 70x24 pixels;
6: a small pile of driftwood planks with seaweed, about 30x16 pixels.
```

Uso no jogo:
- **1–2:** a tábua em que o personagem se agarra; o corpo dele é desenhado por código.
- **3:** os pedaços que ficam boiando no lugar onde a canoa quebrou.
- **4:** os pedaços que chegam na praia junto com o personagem.
- **5:** a canoa quando se desfaz em terra.
- **6:** decoração de praia, também usada como pista de naufrágio de NPCs na 6D.
